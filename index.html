Roll history

    Odds max 2x the line bet. Tap a bet spot to place the selected chip. Line bets go down on the come-out roll; odds go behind the line after a point is set. Come and don’t come bets go down after a point is set, then travel to their own number on the next roll. Come odds are off on a come-out roll (returned if the bet resolves). Place, buy and lay bets are also off on a come-out roll and stay up after a win; buy charges 5% of the bet and lay 5% of the win, taken from winnings.
